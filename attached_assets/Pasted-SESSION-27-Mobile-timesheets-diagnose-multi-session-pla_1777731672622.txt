8. THE FUNDAMENTAL iOS BACKGROUND-LOCATION CONSTRAINTS
   Document these as facts the plan must respect:
   a. iOS limits ~20 active geofences per app, system-wide
   b. iOS requires "Always" permission for background geofence triggers
      ("When in Use" only fires while app is foregrounded)
   c. Geofence triggers fire even when app is backgrounded or killed,
      but the response window is limited
   d. iOS may delay or coalesce geofence events to save battery
   e. Geofences typically need 100m+ radius for reliable triggering
      (smaller radii get unreliable fast)
   f. Cold-start from a geofence event: app gets ~10-30 seconds to
      respond before iOS suspends it
   
   Confirm by reviewing Apple's "Region Monitoring" docs (don't fetch,
   just confirm you know these constraints).
